- For 'https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly':
"Required to retrieve private channel performance metrics such as Click-Through Rate (CTR), Average View Duration (AVD), and traffic source breakdown for the creator's own published videos."

- For 'https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload':
"Required to publish optimized video drafts, custom thumbnails, and structured descriptions directly to the creator's own YouTube channel from the pre-upload staging workflow."
